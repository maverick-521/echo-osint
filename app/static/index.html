Old usernames leave echoes. This finds them — and tells you where your details have leaked.

What each mode does

X history — every former @username with dates, display names, bios, avatars and banners, from public archives.

Multi-platform — emails, ig: / tiktok: / gh: / reddit: / yt: / tg: / bsky: handles or profile links across ~23 public sites, with confidence labels.

Leak check — is an email, phone or username in known data breaches? Free public sources only, metadata only: no leaked passwords are ever downloaded or stored.

The Internet Archive often rate-limits (HTTP 429); runs are time-boxed and keep what they got. Only public, free sources — no logins, no paid databases.
